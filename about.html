The Company

New construction, and nothing else.

JDP Plumbing was started by James D. Porter after a decade running rough-in crews for larger mechanical contractors around the Central Valley. He kept hearing the same complaint from builders: plumbers who split their week between service calls and new construction, and new construction lost every time.

So JDP was built around a different rule — no repair work, no emergency calls, no fixture upgrades on someone's existing bathroom. Just new builds, on the schedule the general contractor actually needs. That focus is why superintendents can put a rough-in date on the board and trust it holds.

Today JDP runs dedicated crews for residential tract work and commercial tenant improvements, with James still on-site reviewing plan sets and walking rough-in before it gets covered.

How We Work

Three rules we don't bend

Plan before pipe

Every job starts with James or a lead reading the full plan set — not just the plumbing sheet — so conflicts get caught before rough-in, not after.

Your calendar wins

We size the crew to the phase. A fourteen-lot tract gets a bigger crew on rough-in week because your framing crew is waiting either way.

Inspection ready, first time

We walk our own rough-in against code before the inspector does. Fewer re-inspections means fewer days added to your build schedule.

The Process

From plan set to final walkthrough

1

Bid the plan set

2

3

Rough-in, top-out, trim
