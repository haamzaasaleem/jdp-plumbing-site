Scope of work

Four lanes of work, all of it new construction. No repairs, no emergency calls — just the plumbing that goes in before the walls close up.

01 — Residential

Residential New Construction

Full rough-in through finish for single-family tracts, custom homes, and infill lots — sized to your framing schedule, lot by lot.

  • Underground DWV and water service before slab pour
  • Wall and ceiling rough-in coordinated with framing sign-off
  • Gas line rough-in for range, dryer, and water heater
  • Top-out and pressure testing ahead of inspection
  • Multi-lot crews scaled to tract phasing
02 — Commercial

Commercial New Construction

Ground-up and tenant-improvement plumbing for offices, retail suites, and light commercial buildings — laid out to match your MEP set.

  • Restroom core and break-room rough-in for TI build-outs
  • Coordination with HVAC and electrical to avoid conflicts
  • Grease waste and floor drain layout for retail/food spaces
  • Domestic water and sanitary mains from point of connection
  • Scheduling built around your general contractor's critical path
03 — Finish Work

Fixture & Finish Installation

The final-stage work that gets a unit to a clean walkthrough — set, connected, tested, and photographed.

  • Tub, shower, and sink set with trim and hardware
  • Water heater installation — tank and tankless
  • Gas appliance hookups: range, dryer, water heater
  • Fixture testing and punch-list walkthrough with super
  • Move-in ready sign-off, unit by unit
04 — Planning

Plan Review & Layout

Before a single pipe goes in, we read the full plan set and lay out DWV and supply runs to match code and the architect's intent.

  • Plan set review across plumbing, framing, and MEP sheets
  • Conflict flagging before rough-in begins
  • Fixture-unit sizing and vent layout to code
  • Bid breakdown by phase: rough-in, top-out, trim
  • Pre-inspection walk of every rough-in before cover
